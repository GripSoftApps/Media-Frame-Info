Media Frame

Turn your tablet into a beautiful Now Playing display

Media Frame transforms your tablet into a customizable "Now Playing" display for music, movies, TV shows, and gaming. Designed for wall mounting, frames, or media furniture setups.

Highlights

  • Beautiful, fully customizable Now Playing display.
  • Large artwork, readable text, and fullscreen animations.
  • Works with Roon, Spotify, Apple TV, Google Cast, WiiM, Home Assistant, Naim (experimental), Eversolo (experimental).
  • Built-in microphone identification via ShazamKit — perfect for vinyl setups.
  • Rich metadata and artwork for music, movies, and TV shows.
  • Gaming dashboard with FPS, CPU, GPU and RTSS integration.
  • Idle mode with artwork slideshows, clocks and visuals.
  • Adaptive brightness tuned for real-world room environments.
  • Night Mode for automatic screen off scheduling.
  • Custom view area scaling for frames and enclosures.
  • Kiosk Mode for a clean appliance-like experience.
  • Web-based control and multi-device sync.
  • No ads. No subscriptions.
  • Privacy-first: mostly local network operation.
