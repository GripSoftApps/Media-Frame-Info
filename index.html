Before you press play

Good to know.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?+

Media Frame is currently available only for Android phones, tablets and Android TV OS. A version for iOS / Apple devices is planned.

What do I need?+

An Android tablet, Android phone or device running Android TV OS. Install Media Frame from Google Play. For Music Recognition, use a phone or tablet with a microphone and internet. For connected sources, keep your screen and player on the same home network.

Do I need a special music system?+

No. Music Recognition can listen to records, CDs, radio or music from a speaker. It identifies recorded music the microphone can hear, so results depend on the recording, volume and background noise. You can add direct integrations later.

Does Music Recognition work on Android TV?+

Music Recognition is available on Android phones and tablets, not the Android TV version. On Android TV, use supported connected players and services instead. Some tablet-specific display features also differ on TV.

Is Media Frame a music player?+

Media Frame is a companion display for what you are listening to or watching. Your existing system plays the music. Media Frame adds the artwork, information and visuals, with playback controls for supported integrations. Matching music-video backgrounds are optional and depend on availability.

How does the free trial work?+

Download the app and try it free for 14 days. After the trial, a one-time purchase unlocks continued use. There is no subscription or automatic charge. The current price is shown in the app / Google Play.

Can I change the look from my phone?+

Yes. Enable Web control in Media Frame, then open the address shown in the app using a browser on your local network. Adjust the layout and settings from your phone, tablet or computer.
